Photos on gifts and gadgets

We print your photos on mugs, T-shirts, puzzles, magnets, cushions and many other gift items. We help you choose the product, prepare files and layouts, and check preview and colour before production.

Looking for a personalised gift idea in Pietragalla and the province of Potenza? With Digital Foto Video you can turn your images into unique gadgets: cotton T-shirts for men and women, smartphone cases, puzzles in various sizes, vintage and heart-shaped magnets, satin, canvas and velvet cushions, breakfast mugs, placemats, throws and Christmas decorations. Every item is carefully printed and previewed before production: perfect for birthdays, weddings, corporate events and small promotional runs.
