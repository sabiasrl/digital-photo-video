About us

Professional photography and video services in the Pietragalla area, in the province of Potenza.

Digital Foto Video is based in the San Nicola Industrial Area (Piano San Nicola), with easy access for clients from nearby towns. We offer solutions for individuals and small businesses: from event coverage to promotional campaigns, with modern equipment and care for the final result.

We work with anyone looking for a personalised service and with local businesses that need professional visual content, in line with the growing demand for digital imaging and multimedia production across Basilicata.

Services

Digital photography, video, printing, gadgets, websites and social media: an overview of the services we offer.

Professional photography

Studio and on-location portraits, still life for catalogues and social media, and tailored services for individuals and businesses in Basilicata. We support you with lighting, framing and set-up to deliver sharp images that match the message you want to communicate.

Video services

Filming, editing and titles for business presentations, social reels and campaigns, tutorials and local promotional content. We deliver files to your required specs (web, projection or lightweight master) so the material is ready to use without further conversion.

ID photos

ID photos for documents and applications with format, background and lighting consistent with institutional requirements. We pay attention to proportions and expression to reduce the risk of rejection in person or when uploading online.

Photo printing

Printing from digital files on glossy, matte or fine-art papers, in multiple sizes and weights. Before the final run you can review a proof, margins and colour so you can choose the finish best suited to albums, walls or promotional materials.

Custom printing services

We offer custom printing on a range of items and materials for individuals, events, businesses and promotions. We create made-to-order products with your images, logos, text and graphics, with care for detail and solutions for every need. Support for personalisation, gift ideas, promotional materials and items for personal or professional use.

Photos on gifts and gadgets

Your images on mugs, T-shirts, calendars, phone cases, puzzles, cushions and other personalised gadgets. We help you choose the product, prepare files and layouts, and check preview and colour before production: ideal for gifts, event keepsakes and small promotional runs.

All gadgets

Photo and video post-production

Colour balance, exposure correction, distraction clean-up and light retouching; for video, cuts, pacing and colour consistency with photos from the same project. Useful when you need a uniform look across content shot at different times or under mixed lighting.

Events and ceremonies

Coverage of weddings, civil and religious ceremonies, private parties and corporate events with a discreet approach and agreed timings. From a curated selection delivery to an album or highlight video, based on what you need to preserve and tell the story of the day.

Aerial drone filming

Aerial shots for property, landscapes, outdoor events and local storytelling, in compliance with applicable regulations. Can be combined with ground footage to add context and depth to catalogues, listings or social and website content.

Frames

Classic and modern frames, mounts and finishing work to showcase prints, certificates and keepsakes for home, office or shop windows. Help choosing proportions and colours so the subject stays central without competing with the frame.

Website design

Websites for individuals, professionals and local businesses: company presentation, services, photo galleries and contact details, with responsive design and mobile optimisation. From page structure to going live, we guide you step by step to a clear, updatable digital presence.

Social media and blogging

Content for Facebook, Instagram and blogs: photos, videos, captions and articles that showcase services, events and behind-the-scenes moments. Editorial calendar planning and regular updates to keep an online presence that is consistent, active and recognisable.

Photos on gifts and gadgets

Mugs, T-shirts, puzzles, magnets, cushions, throws and many other items personalised with your photos. Ideal for gifts, events and promotions.
